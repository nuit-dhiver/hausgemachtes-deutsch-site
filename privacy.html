Privacy Policy

Hausgemachtes Deutsch · Last updated 25 November 2026

Hausgemachtes Deutsch is a flashcard app for learning German. It is made by Sayed Mohammad Hossein Emami (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with your data.

The short version

The app does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are, the app saves your study progress on your iPhone or iPad: which words you have seen and learned, your streaks, the lists you make, your goal and daily pace, and your settings. This information never leaves your device through the app. It is included in your device’s own backups (iCloud or computer backups) if you have those turned on, under Apple’s terms. Deleting the app deletes it.

Pronunciation

Words and example sentences are read aloud by the text-to-speech voices built into iOS. This happens on your device; no audio or text is sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
