Common questions

How do I answer a card?
Tap the card to see its meaning. Swipe right if you knew it and left if you didn’t. Undo brings the last card back for a few seconds after each swipe.

When is a word learned?
A word you get right the first time you see it is learned at once. A word you missed needs three right answers in a row. Practice brings back the words that were hard for you until you know them five times in a row.

Can I change my goal or daily pace?
Yes, in Settings or with Change on the Progress tab. Your progress is kept when you change them.

The voice sounds robotic.
The app uses the German voice installed on your device. In the Settings app, go to Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices › German and download an Enhanced or Premium voice. The app uses it as soon as it is there.

How do I switch the app to Persian or English?
Open the Settings app, then Hausgemacht › Language. The language of the meanings is set separately, in the app’s own Settings.

Is my progress backed up?
It is saved on your device and included in your device’s iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
